# Rubi

Rubi is an iPhone app that reads a photo of a printed Japanese page and shows it with furigana. Tap a word for its reading and meaning, save it, and review it later. Everything runs on the iPhone. There is no account and no network use.

- [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md)
- Support: [open an issue](https://github.com/K3NTAW/rubi-app/issues/new) with your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Please do not attach photos of pages you do not have the right to share.

## Dictionary data

The dictionary Rubi ships is published here without any copy protection, so anyone can reuse it under its licence. Download `dictionary.sqlite.zip` from [Releases](https://github.com/K3NTAW/rubi-app/releases).

It is a SQLite file built from JMdict, JMnedict and KANJIDIC2 (EDRDG), JmdictFurigana, and the JLPT levels in yomitan-jlpt-vocab.
