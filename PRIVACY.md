# Rubi privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-06.

Rubi does not collect any data about you.

- **No account, no network.** Rubi makes no network connections. Nothing you scan, paste, share or save is sent anywhere.
- **Camera and photos.** The camera is used only to scan pages while the scan screen is open. Photos and pages you import are read on the iPhone.
- **What is stored, and where.** Scans, page photos (if you keep them), saved words, review history and settings are stored only on your iPhone, in Rubi's own storage. Device backups you make through iCloud or a computer may include them, under Apple's terms.
- **Sharing into Rubi.** Text, images and PDFs you share to Rubi from other apps are copied into Rubi's storage on the iPhone.
- **No tracking, no analytics, no ads.** Rubi contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.
- **Crash reports.** If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may pass crash reports to the developer. These come from Apple, not from Rubi.
- **Deleting your data.** Settings, Reset all data deletes everything Rubi stored. Deleting the app does the same.

If this changes, for example if a later version offers to send scans to improve recognition, the app will ask first and this page will be updated.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/K3NTAW/rubi-app/issues/new).
